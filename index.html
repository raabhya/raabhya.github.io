Current research

The Bus Stop Location Problem using Optimization
Undergraduate dissertation · 2026–27 · In progress

Every bus stop is a trade-off. Put stops close together and passengers walk less but buses crawl; spread them out and journeys speed up while some neighbourhoods are left too far from service. My dissertation treats stop placement as an optimization problem and searches for layouts that balance these competing goals.

I'm studying how stop locations can be chosen for Delhi's public transport network, drawing on the literature on facility location and multi-objective optimization, and on recent work applying these methods to real cities.
